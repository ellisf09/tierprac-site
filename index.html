Features

Queues

Queue to fight against the best players in their respective modes, improving your skills and getting you ready for competitive play. There's no other server like it, as others queue you against players that have no clue how to play.

Tournaments

Compete in organized events and prove yourself against the community's best players. They also usually offer prizes, making them a great way to showcase your skills and win rewards.

Performance

Enjoy our heavily optimised practice environment with the best performance and lowest latency. We also have fixes for knockback, projectiles and other issues.

Customization

Personalize your experience with a range of customization options, from tons of duel settings to your own personal preferences.

Community

Learn as you play from feedback and support from our community of the best players in their modes. You can't get that anywhere else other than tier practice.

Parties

Play with an unlimited number of people in party fights, with splits, FFAs and more in our wide range of kits and maps.

READY TO COMPETE?

How to join Tier Practice

Tier Practice is for verified LT3+ players. Follow these steps to get started:

  1. Meet the requirement. You must be LT3 or higher in an MCTiers, Subtiers, or PvPTiers mode. Alternatively, you can boost the Discord server to get access, along with trims as a special thank you!
  2. Add the server. Enter tierprac.xyz as the server address.
  3. Join the community. Check Discord for verification, events, and updates.

Meet the requirements, but unable to join? Make a ticket in the discord.
